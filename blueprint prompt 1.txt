Below is a **complete V1 blueprint** for your free Flatmate-style website. The goal is to launch something focused, useful, and scalable to an Android app later.

# 1. Define the platform clearly

The platform should primarily solve:

### A. I have a place

> I have a room/flat and need a flatmate.

### B. I need a place

> I am looking for a room/flat/flatmate.

Initially, **do not try to become NoBroker + Housing + PG + hotel booking all at once**.

Your core positioning:

> **Find rooms, flatmates and shared accommodation for free. Chat directly. No contact unlock charges.**

---

# 2. User types

Keep only **one User table**. Don't create separate account systems.

A user can have multiple roles:

* Looking for accommodation
* Have accommodation
* Both

For example, someone may have a 3BHK with one vacant room while also looking to move somewhere else.

---

# 5. Post/listing types

Your main `listings` table can support multiple types.

## V1 listing types

### 1. Room Available

> I have a vacant room and need a flatmate.

### 2. Flat Available

> I have an entire flat/property available for sharing or renting.

### 3. Looking for Room

> I need a room/shared accommodation.

### 4. Looking for Flatmate

This can overlap with Room Available, so technically you can combine these.

My recommendation for UI:

### When clicking **Post**

Ask:

**What are you looking to do?**

🏠 **I have a room/place**
Find a flatmate or tenant.

🔍 **I'm looking for a place**
Find a room, flat or flatmate.

This keeps the system simple.

# 15. Listing lifecycle

This is extremely important to prevent dead listings.

Suggested system:

```text
Draft
 ↓
Published
 ↓
Active
 ↓
Expiring Soon
 ↓
Expired
```

Ask after 25–30 days:

> Is this listing still available?

Buttons:

**Yes, keep active**
**No, mark as closed**

If no response, automatically expire it.

# 19. Recommended database structure

At a high level:

```text
users
user_profiles
user_preferences

listings
listing_photos
listing_locations
listing_amenities
listing_preferences

conversations
conversation_participants
messages
message_attachments

saved_listings
notifications

reports
blocked_users

verification_requests

cities
localities
```

### Important principle

Do not create separate tables like:

```text
room_seekers
flat_seekers
property_owners
flatmate_users
```

Use one `users` table and connect users to listings/profiles.

# 21. Matching system — don't build AI initially

You can create a simple matching score.

Example:

```text
Location match     40%
Budget match       25%
Move-in date       15%
Gender preference  10%
Lifestyle          10%
```

Then display:

> **85% Match**

Later you can improve it with smarter recommendations.

For V1, normal database filtering is enough.

# 22. Ads strategy

Since your platform will remain free:

## Google Ads

Potential locations:

### Desktop

* Between listing cards
* Bottom of listing page
* Sidebar

### Mobile

* Between results
* Bottom sections

### Never place ads:

* Inside chat messages
* Directly over important buttons
* In a way that causes accidental clicks

Initially, I would focus on **getting real users before aggressively optimizing ad revenue**. Low traffic usually means very little ad revenue.

Avoid wasting time on:

* AI matching
* Video calling
* Payment gateway
* Android app
* iOS app
* ID verification automation
* Complex recommendation engine
* Maps with advanced route calculation
* PG management
